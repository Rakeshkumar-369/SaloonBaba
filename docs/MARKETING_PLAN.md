# SaloonBaba — Go-to-Market Plan

This plan turns the product blueprint into a launch: who we sell to first, what we say, how we price, which channels we use, and the numbers we track. It assumes a small founding team (2–4 people) launching in Chennai and Bengaluru, then expanding to tier-2 cities in Tamil Nadu and Karnataka.

---

## 1. Positioning

**One line:** SaloonBaba is the operating system for Indian salons — it fills empty chairs, brings first-time clients back, pays staff fairly and shows true daily profit.

**For** salon owners and franchise brands **who** are busy but not profitable, **SaloonBaba is** a Salon OS plus customer, staff and franchise apps **that** measures and moves the numbers that decide salon profit. **Unlike** billing software (records the past) or listing apps like Urban Company or District (sell the customer to the highest bidder), **we** run the salon itself and keep the client relationship with the salon.

### Proof points we can say on day one

| Claim | Source / mechanism in the product |
|---|---|
| "55% of your first-time clients never come back." | Industry benchmark (Boulevard 2023); our KPI home measures *your* number on day one. |
| "Clients who book online return twice as often." | 78% vs 39% (Boulevard 2023); our booking link + WhatsApp bot moves walk-ins online. |
| "Deposits cut no-shows almost in half." | 47% (industry data); UPI deposits are built into booking. |
| "Payroll should be 30–35% of revenue." | Live payroll % on the owner's home screen. |
| "Your stylists will stop asking 'how much did I earn?'" | Live earnings wallet in the employee app. |

## 2. Ideal customers (in order)

| # | Segment | Size of prize | Why they buy | First offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Independent premium & mid-market salons (4–12 chairs)** in Chennai & Bengaluru | Highest volume; decision maker is the owner | Retention, payroll disputes, cash leakage, no P&L | Growth plan, free 30-day pilot with data migration |
| 2 | **Multi-outlet owners (2–10 outlets)** including franchisees | Bigger ACV, quick referrals | Rollup, P&L after royalty, loss prevention | Pro plan, outlet #1 free for 3 months |
| 3 | **Regional franchise brands (10–200 outlets)** | Large contracts, network effects for the customer app | Royalty disputes, SOP drift, audits, lead routing | Franchise console pilot on 5 outlets |
| 4 | **Barbershops & women-only salons** | Huge count, low ticket | Walk-in queue ("Seat in 15 min"), WhatsApp booking | Starter (free) → Growth upsell |
| 5 | **Vendors** (distributors, brands, academies, equipment) | Monetise once we hold salon data | Sell-through data, collections, promotions | Phase 2 (after ~300 active salons) |

**Not now:** at-home service aggregators, spas inside hotels, single-chair parlours without a smartphone at the desk.

## 3. Pricing & packaging

| Plan | Price (ex-GST) | Who | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Free forever | 1–3 chair salons | Calendar & queue, GST billing on WhatsApp, client cards, listing on the customer app |
| **Growth** | ₹1,499 / outlet / month | Most salons | + retention engine & automations, payroll & incentives, inventory with recipes, daily P&L, cash closing, Tally export, employee app |
| **Pro** | ₹2,999 / outlet / month | Multi-outlet owners | + multi-outlet rollup, loss prevention, campaign attribution, hygiene/SOP, priority support |
| **Franchise** | Custom (₹1,999–2,499 / outlet + HQ fee) | Brands | + network dashboard, royalty engine, central catalogue, audits, lead routing, training |

**Usage-based extras:** WhatsApp/SMS campaign messages at cost + 20%; payment gateway at standard MDR (UPI free to the salon where possible).
**Customer side:** booking is free for customers; **SaloonBaba Gold** at ₹299 / 90 days (priority weekend slots, member prices at partner salons) is the consumer revenue line.
**Annual prepay:** 2 months free. **Pilot rule:** every paid plan starts with a 30-day free pilot after data migration — no card needed.

**Unit economics target (Growth):** ₹1,499 MRR, < ₹6,000 CAC (field + inside sales), payback < 4 months, gross margin > 80%.

## 4. Channels

### 4.1 Field + WhatsApp-first inside sales (primary, months 0–6)
- 2 field reps per city walk the salon high streets (Anna Nagar, T Nagar, Velachery, Adyar, Indiranagar, Koramangala, HSR, Jayanagar) with the demo on a phone.
- Every visit ends with the owner's WhatsApp number → follow-up sequence (Day 0 demo video, Day 2 case numbers, Day 5 pilot offer).
- **Hook:** "Give us your last 3 months of bills (Excel or billing export) — in 20 minutes we will show your 2nd-visit return rate, dead hours and payroll %." This is free and creates urgency from their own numbers.

### 4.2 Distributor partnerships (highest leverage)
- Professional product distributors visit 300–1,000 salons each, every month. Offer them the vendor portal free plus a referral fee (one month of subscription per converted salon).
- Their pitch: "Order from me in one tap when stock is low" — which requires the salon to be on SaloonBaba.

### 4.3 Franchise brands (top-down)
- Approach regional brands with 10–200 outlets; lead with the **royalty engine** (no more under-reporting disputes) and **digital audits**.
- One signed brand brings all its outlets and populates the customer app in a city.

### 4.4 Academies & associations
- Partner with beauty academies (free course marketplace listing) and state salon associations / beautician unions for workshops: "How to raise your 2nd-visit rate from 45% to 60%".

### 4.5 Consumer side (from month 4, once ≥ 50 salons in a city)
- Every salon's booking link in Instagram bio and Google Business Profile (built into Settings → Booking channels).
- Instagram/Reels content: "Seat in 15 minutes — no waiting", stylist portfolios, festival looks.
- Off-peak happy hours pushed to nearby customers fill dead hours — the salon sees results within a week, which drives word of mouth among owners.
- Referral: friend gets 15% off, referrer gets ₹200 wallet credit (already in product).

### 4.6 Content & SEO
- City pages: "Best salons in Anna Nagar with live wait time".
- Owner blog/YouTube in Tamil, Kannada, Hindi: payroll slabs that work, retail without being pushy, GST for salons, how to price packages.

## 6. Sales demo script (20 minutes)

1. **Their numbers first (5 min).** Upload their bills → show *Home*: revenue, 2nd-visit return vs 45%/70% benchmark, payroll %, dead hours heat-map. Ask: "What would 10 more returning clients a week be worth?"
2. **A day at the front desk (5 min).** Calendar + walk-in queue → bill with split UPI/cash → owner OTP for a big discount → rebook prompt. "Every service is billed, every discount is approved."
3. **Staff (3 min).** Employee app in their language: live earnings, selfie check-in, career ladder. "Stylists stay where they can see their future."
4. **Money (4 min).** P&L after rent, EB, salary, product (and royalty); loss prevention trail; cash closing.
5. **Customers (3 min).** Their salon on the customer app with "Seat in 15 min", prices including GST, verified reviews. Close: "Pilot free for 30 days — we migrate everything this week."

### Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "We already have billing software." | Keep it for a month if you like — we import your data. Billing records the past; SaloonBaba moves 2nd-visit return, no-shows and payroll %, and runs staff pay and stock. |
| "My staff won't use apps." | The employee app answers their favourite question — "how much did I earn today?" — in Tamil/Hindi/Kannada. Check-in takes 5 seconds. |
| "Customers won't download an app." | They don't have to: a link on Instagram/Google and your WhatsApp number handle bookings. |
| "Aggregators take commission." | We don't. Repeat bookings are free and the client belongs to you. |
| "Too expensive." | One recovered no-show or one extra returning client a week covers the Growth plan. The pilot is free. |
| "My data is private." | It is yours: masked numbers for staff, export any time, and vendors only see anonymised, consented aggregates. |

## 8. Brand & messaging

- **Voice:** practical, numbers-first, respectful of the craft. Talk like a smart salon manager, not like a tech company.
- **Visual:** violet-to-pink gradient, rounded cards, real stylists and real salons (never stock models), Indian names and rupee amounts everywhere.
- **Taglines to test:** "Fill every chair. Keep every client." · "Busy is not the same as profitable." · "Seat in 15 minutes."
- **Languages:** English + Tamil + Kannada at launch; Hindi, Telugu, Bengali as we expand.

## 9. Phase 2 — vendor revenue (after ~300 active salons)

Once usage and sales data exist across hundreds of salons, the vendor portal becomes the highest-margin business:

| Product | Who pays | Model |
|---|---|---|
| One-tap reordering + storefront | Distributors | 1–2% commission on orders |
| Credit & collections, NBFC working-capital loans | Distributors, lenders | Fee per collection / loan origination |
| Sell-through & usage insights | Brands | ₹25k–1L / month subscription |
| Targeted promotions & sampling | Brands | Pay per campaign / per claim |
| Education marketplace | Academies, brands | Revenue share on paid courses |
| Equipment service & AMC leads | Equipment suppliers | Per lead / subscription |
| Authenticity codes | Brands | Per code printed |

The flywheel: **more salons on the OS → better live availability for customers → more online bookings → more data → vendors pay → salon price stays low.**
